Lecturer Profile

Nino Latsabidze

Lecturer · Teacher Trainer · Educational Researcher
Nino Latsabidze is a lecturer, teacher trainer, and educational researcher specializing in the use of artificial intelligence in teaching and learning. She teaches at Ilia State University, Business and Technology University, and Sulkhan-Saba Orbeliani University, where she delivers courses on AI applications in language education. She also provides professional development training for academic staff on integrating AI into teaching practices. Nino is currently pursuing a PhD in Education at Ilia State University, focusing on the impact of AI feedback on students’ academic writing skills.
