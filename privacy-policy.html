Communication: To send technical updates and information regarding the "Innovation Challenge" project.

Marketing & Lead Generation: To provide information about upcoming B2B services and tailored AI training.

Compliance & Reporting: To fulfill mandatory reporting and monitoring obligations to our donors, UNDP and the EU.

Your Consent: Provided during registration.

Contractual Obligation: To provide the educational services you registered for.

Legitimate Interest: To fulfill project monitoring requirements by the UNDP.

We do not sell your personal data. However, your information may be shared with:

7. International Data Transfers

Personal data collected from users in the European Union (Austria) will be processed in Georgia. We ensure that such transfers comply with the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection and align with European data protection principles to ensure an adequate level of security.

We store your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the project's goals. Financial and programmatic records, which may include participant lists, are kept for 3 years following the expiration of the agreement with UNDP for audit purposes.

9. Your Rights

Under Georgian law and European standards (GDPR), you have the right to:

Request Deletion of your data ("Right to be forgotten").

To exercise these rights, please contact us at theartofAI@geolab.edu.ge

GeoLab maintains appropriate technical and organizational security plans to protect your data from unauthorized access or destruction, in accordance with the security directives of our agreement with UNDP.
